# The lines for the day

One short line after the check-in each day, chosen by the date
(`src/app/lines.ts`). The lines are walked in one fixed shuffled order, as
the sea facts are, so none comes back until all twenty have had their day.
A line that a moment of the same day already says (below) is not said
twice: the check-in shows the one half a cycle away instead.

The author chose every line and approved its words; they are not changed
in the code. The author's own lines carry no name. A line someone else
said carries their name in lowercase, and comes only from a translation in
the public domain, named here.

## The author's

1. it always passes. it has always passed.
2. one day you might not get to try this. let’s do it while we can.
3. even when it looks impossible: i’ll try. i’ll get up.
4. not feeling it is just weather.
5. worst case isn’t trying and failing. it’s never trying.
6. not perfect. still moving.
7. compare day one with today.
8. what you do today is not who you are.
9. it goes best when you just do it, not knowing how.
10. start light. stay. it shows.
11. it seems small. it adds up.
12. success is a state of mind, waiting for action.
13. if you get there one day, who do you want next to you?
14. used to think about success. now more about connection.
15. a pawn can become anything if it never stops moving forward.
